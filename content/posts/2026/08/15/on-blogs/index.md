Blogs as a concept are incredibly fascinating to me. To be more precise, the concepts around personal writing, digital or otherwise, are incredibly fascinating to me.

Even if they might be published for the public or a niche circle of people, the idea of writing on things about your life for some subset of the world to see is one that largely motivates why I write this blog. Maybe it's the sort of vulnerability (however measured) that could come with that, or the way it allows the writer to invite others to take a peek into their life - if only a little bit. I'm not solely referring to introspective writings much like what you might find here, but including the mundane things that are so ephemeral that they lose any relevance a week later. However, as much as I find public writing in this way a sort of romantic act, I want to talk less about the content and more about the mechanisms that facilitated them over the decades - particularly online.
